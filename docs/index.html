How BAIX works

  • Only revenue a company itself labels as AI counts — not AI spending, AI-powered products, deal wins or company names.
  • Universe screened from all 2,620 NSE-listed equities down to companies with disclosed AI revenue.
  • Reviewed twice a year, with cut-offs on SEBI's results deadlines (30 May, 14 November).
